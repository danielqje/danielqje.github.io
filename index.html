About Me

I am an undergraduate student at San Diego State University (SDSU) pursuing a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Astronomy with a Minor in Mathematics. My interests span galaxies, stellar populations, compact objects, observational astronomy, and data analysis, with a growing interest in using spectroscopy and scientific computing to study astrophysical systems.

My current research experience has focused on stellar absorption features in elliptical galaxy spectra, particularly the Na I D doublet and how stellar populations can affect interpretations of gas inflows. I enjoy combining astronomy, physics, mathematics, and Python to better understand the physical processes behind what we observe in the universe.

Outside of my current research, I'm especially fascinated by neutron stars, black holes, and the evolution of galaxies. My long-term goal is to continue into graduate research in astronomy or astrophysics and contribute to observational and computational research.
