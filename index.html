About Me

Since a little kid I always had an interest in the great heavens, something about the vastness of space and all the celestial objects staring back at me from the night sky intrigued me. In elementary school when we'd go to the library to pick a book I always chose space books, especially a book on Jupiter (my favorite planet). I recall that my biggest fascination, the Moon, as a child and even teenager, always seemed to follow me everywhere I went and most noticeable while I was in the backseat of a driving car and I always wondered, "how is it follow me everywhere I go and why does its velocity change to match mine", well now I know it's because its enormous distance creates negligible parallax so my changing position produces almost no apparent angular shift in the sky. I couldn't even begin to tell you how starstruck I feel looking at nebulae images.

I am an undergraduate student at San Diego State University (SDSU) pursuing a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Astronomy with a Minor in Mathematics. My interests span galaxies, stellar populations, compact objects, observational astronomy, research, and data analysis, with a growing interest in using spectroscopy and scientific computing to study astrophysical systems.
