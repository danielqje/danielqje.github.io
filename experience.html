Mission 2: Idunn Lunar Mission

NASA NCAS | 2025

Collaborated with a team to design Idunn, a conceptual robotic mission to the Moon. The project simulated the scientific, engineering, financial, and operational planning involved in developing a NASA mission proposal.

Served as an Instrument Engineer and helped evaluate scientific instruments based on their research capabilities, cost, mass, power requirements, and compatibility with the mission’s scientific objectives. Our team also developed an entry, descent, and landing plan, created a mission schedule, managed a limited project budget, evaluated technical risks, and presented the completed mission proposal.

Project Responsibilities

  • Evaluated scientific instruments for a conceptual lunar mission.
  • Compared instrument cost, mass, power use, and scientific value.
  • Contributed to the entry, descent, and landing plan.
  • Worked within mission budget and scheduling constraints.
  • Documented technical risks and proposed mitigation strategies.
  • Collaborated on the mission poster, progress reports, written materials, and final presentation.

Skills Developed

Systems engineering, scientific instrument evaluation, mission planning, technical communication, budgeting, risk assessment, teamwork, and collaborative problem-solving.
