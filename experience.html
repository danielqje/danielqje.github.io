Mission 2

Explore: Project Idunn Lunar Mission

Collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to develop Project Idunn, a conceptual lunar mission focused on sustainable food production for long-duration human habitation. The project simulated the scientific, engineering, financial, and operational planning involved in developing a NASA mission proposal.

As an Instrument Engineer, I researched, compared, and justified scientific equipment using cost, mass, power draw, scientific return, safety, and mission relevance. I proposed a Life Sciences Laboratory, high-resolution cameras, a color stereo camera, a radiation sensor, and an Advanced Resistive Exercise Device to support food safety, astronaut health, and mission sustainability.

Project Responsibilities

  • Developed an instrument proposal within the mission’s cost, mass, power, and scientific-return constraints.
  • Researched spaceflight, astronaut nutrition, food safety, and environmental hazards to justify instrument selections.
  • Contributed to the lunar entry, descent, and landing plan.
  • Collaborated on the mission schedule, budget, risk decisions, annotated bibliography, research poster, and written materials.
  • Worked with the team to revise the mission after simulation events, including an unsuccessful rocket test.
