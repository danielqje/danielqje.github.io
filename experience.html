NASA Community College Aerospace Scholars

I participated in NASA Community College Aerospace Scholars (NCAS), an academic program that introduced students to NASA missions, scientific research, engineering concepts, and collaborative mission planning.

Role

Instrument Engineer

As the Instrument Engineer for my team, I focused on evaluating scientific instruments and considering how instrument capabilities supported the scientific goals of our mission concept.

Mission Focus

Our work focused on the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) under NASA's Science Mission Directorate, with an emphasis on galactic astronomy and the scientific capabilities required to investigate astronomical systems.

Project Experience

  • Worked collaboratively as part of a simulated NASA mission-development team
  • Served as the team's Instrument Engineer
  • Researched the James Webb Space Telescope and its scientific capabilities
  • Investigated topics related to galactic astronomy
  • Evaluated scientific instruments through an instrument trade study
  • Considered mission requirements, engineering constraints, scientific objectives, risk, and cost
  • Contributed to mission planning and the development of team project materials
  • Participated in the development of an entry, descent, and landing planning exercise as part of the broader NCAS mission-design experience
  • Developed and presented a concise mission pitch
  • Used scientific literature and NASA resources to support research and project decisions

Skills Developed

Scientific Research Mission Planning Instrument Analysis Technical Communication Team Collaboration Trade Studies Risk Analysis Cost Considerations Presentation

Experience & Takeaway

NCAS gave me experience approaching scientific and engineering problems from a NASA mission-development perspective. The program strengthened my understanding of how science objectives, instrumentation, engineering constraints, teamwork, and communication come together in the development of a space mission.

The experience also helped strengthen my interest in astronomy, astrophysics, scientific research, and opportunities within NASA and the broader space-science community.
