Research

Stellar Na I D Absorption in Elliptical Galaxies

Summer Research Program - STARTAstro — University of California San Diego (UCSD) & San Diego State University (SDSU)

My current research investigates how stellar populations contribute to the Na I D absorption feature in elliptical galaxy spectra and how this absorption can affect the interpretation of gas inflows.

Research Focus

Na I D absorption can be produced by both stars and interstellar gas. Understanding the stellar contribution is therefore important when using Na I D to identify signatures of gas moving into or out of galaxies.

Methods

  • Analyzed model stellar population spectra
  • Examined changes in Na I D with varying alpha-element abundances
  • Performed continuum normalization around the Na I D region
  • Modeled the Na I D doublet using double-Gaussian fits
  • Calculated the ratio between the normalized spectrum and optimized Gaussian fit
  • Used ratio residuals to identify excess absorption not captured by the stellars model
  • Compared observed spectral absorption with fitted models
  • Used Python for spectral analysis and visualization
